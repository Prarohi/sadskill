# Checklist: 15-Point Launch Legal & Compliance

A checklist ensuring software applications meet standard global data privacy and regulatory compliance requirements before launch.

---

## 📋 Compliance Checkpoints

1. **Privacy Policy**: A comprehensive, publicly accessible Privacy Policy detailing collected data, processing rationale, and retention policies.
2. **Terms of Service**: Clear Terms of Service outlining acceptable use, user obligations, intellectual property rights, and limitation of liability.
3. **Cookie Consent & Banner**: Explicit consent mechanism for non-essential tracking cookies (GDPR/ePrivacy compliance).
4. **Data Deletion Mechanism**: Straightforward workflow allowing users to request account deletion and removal of personal identifiable information (GDPR Right to Erasure / CCPA).
5. **Data Export Capability**: Self-serve or support workflow for data portability (GDPR Article 20).
6. **Subprocessor Disclosure**: List of third-party vendors and cloud providers processing customer data (AWS, Stripe, Supabase, OpenAI, etc.).
7. **Age Restrictions & Verification**: Clear statement of minimum age requirements (COPPA compliance).
8. **Security Incident Contact**: Security vulnerability disclosure policy or dedicated `security@` contact channel.
9. **Commercial Email Compliance**: Functional unsubscribe link and physical mailing address in transactional/marketing emails (CAN-SPAM / CASL).
10. **Payment & Billing Disclosure**: Explicit recurring subscription terms, renewal dates, and cancellation procedures before charging payment cards.
11. **Open Source License Attribution**: Comprehensive acknowledgment of third-party open-source components and licenses used in the product.
12. **Audit Logging & Access Control**: Access to customer production data restricted via role-based access controls and logged for auditing.
13. **Data Encryption Standards**: Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+ / TLS 1.3) across all customer-facing systems.
14. **Cross-Border Transfer Protections**: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent transfer mechanisms if moving EU customer data abroad.
15. **Accessibility Conformance**: Basic ADA / Section 508 legal compliance avoiding discriminatory accessibility barriers in commercial workflows.
